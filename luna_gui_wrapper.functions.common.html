luna_gui_wrapper.functions.common module

Application identity and small helpers shared by every layer.

Qt-free: importing this never pulls in PySide6.

luna_gui_wrapper.functions.common.dataset_row(name, ds)[source]

One row of the Inspect tab’s Datasets table, as (text, tooltip) pairs: name, rows, fields, chunks.

Works on anything with h5py’s shape / dtype / chunks, so it is testable on a stand-in. A 1-D table shows its length and its field names; the exact numpy shape and dtype are the tooltips.
